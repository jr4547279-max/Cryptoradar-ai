Entry: 24h gain ≥ threshold, sufficient 24h volume, positive 7d change when available, and sufficient cash. Exit: fixed stop loss, take profit, or 24h reversal. One position per coin. Paper fills use the latest observed market price with a simulated 0.20% transaction fee and 0.15% adverse slippage on both entry and exit. No leverage. Signals use backward-looking snapshots; evidence scores are rule-based and are NOT probabilities of profit. Performance is not backtested or validated.

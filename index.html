The bot return uses the portfolio value at the same start time. This is forward observation, not historical backtesting, and only records scans while the page is open.

Entry: 24h gain ≥ threshold, sufficient 24h volume, positive 7d change when available, and sufficient cash. Exit: fixed stop loss, take profit, or 24h reversal. One position per coin. Paper fills use the latest observed market price with a simulated 0.20% transaction fee and 0.15% adverse slippage on both entry and exit. No leverage. Signals use backward-looking snapshots; evidence scores are rule-based and are NOT probabilities of profit. Performance is not backtested or validated. Daily loss guard blocks new entries but does not liquidate positions or guarantee a loss ceiling.
